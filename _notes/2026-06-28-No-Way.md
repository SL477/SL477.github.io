Many years ago I saw Star Wars Episode 6 Return of the Jedi for the first time.
And it made me wonder, why aren't all movies like this?

I found out that there were two other films in the series and saw them.
Then a year later Star Wars Episode 1 The Phantom Menace came out and I saw that (and Jurassic Park somewhere in the middle).

Since then I've watched hundreds of episodes of Star Trek, Stargate and Star Wars.
And read hundreds of books of those and other sci-fi universes (I want to live in the Culture or Star Trek).
