I started blogging on and off on the 17th of August 2021.
My first post was about a Blood Bowl match I had had, some day I should release the rest of that series (only had them sitting on the harddrive for five years).
My website had been around for a few years by then and had only really been a sort of professional business card.

The blog side started off on Wordpress, roughly from that time.
It united with my main website in late 2022.

My website started off as my answer to one of FreeCodeCamp's assignments.
It first lived on CodePen.
Then it went over to GitHub pages.
I made the terrible mistake of getting rid of Jekyll at first, before eventually restoring it.
I was going to replace the HTML side with a React site and fortunately changed my mind (I have a section of the site on React, a bit on Vue and most on Jekyll).
Maybe at some point I'll move the React part to the Jekyll side.

Originally this was going to be some sort of serious technical blog.
Ruined that with my [first post](https://link477.com/2021/08/17/Blood-Bowl-1st-Match.html)! Now its main focus is just whatever I want to write about.
