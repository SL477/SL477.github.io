Sly O'Hara relaxed in the hideout, discarded boxes of fish and chips littered the floor.
He and his crew had taken over the apartment above a fish and chip shop.
Stanley Jenson was a new member of the crew, he had arrived on the habitat a few months ago and found out the hard way that the first ship to anywhere could see you stranded somewhere that wasn't great.
Chris Carey had been using the crew's van in various street races and it was now a bit faster than it had been before.
Gunner Gunther had been working in the fish and chip shop.
Pretty Mickey had taken up bare knuckle boxing.
Nat the Thumper was another new recruit to the team.
She had been recruited by Pretty Mickey from the boxing ring.

"Right everyone," said Jerome Lighter, he was an underworld fixer who had set up the gang with a few minor jobs before.
He somehow made his casual shirt and corduroy trousers look like a uniform.
"I have a job for you all.
There is a lab on Southend Street, I would like you to break in and steal the data crystals from their server room."
"Sounds simple enough," Nat the Thumper said.
"The client will be paying ten times your normal fee for danger money."
Jerome continued with his false bonhomie.

The van pulled up in the alleyway behind the lab.
The street was quiet, the only unit occupied was the one that Progenitor Laboratory had taken over.
Cameras were mounted around the building and there were bars on the windows.
There were no visible guards on the outside of the building.
Jerome had assured them that the power would be cut to the area.

Nat the Thumper advanced on the back door, her powered sledgehammer in hand.
All five of the breaching team wore balaclavas, just in case the Lab was able to bring legal troubles for them.
Pretty Mickey jumped in and placed an explosive charge against the door, one of the toys from Jerome.

There was a loud bang as it went off, the hydraulic door was now partly open and off its runner.
Nat slammed her sledgehammer against it.
After a few hits there was enough room for the crew to get in.

Two explosives were used on the inner door.
Emergency lighting was on in the rear of the lab.
It appeared to be a storage area.

"What's a genetic research laboratory doing here?"
Gunther asked.
"And why do they need all of these giant boxes?"

The boxes he referred to were the giant crates scattered through the room.
All wired up and gently humming with power.

"I thought this place was supposed to be dead?" Stanley complained.

"Well it doesn't look like these went out."
Sly said.
His silver eyes scanning the room.
"Mickey, guard the exit, make sure we don't get any visitors."

The four of them continued into the building.
The server room was in the middle of the building.
Stanley had taken Mickey's remaining explosive charge, Sly didn't trust the newbie to not run away.
A mixture of the explosive and the sledgehammer persuaded the server room door to open.

"Looks like our target is here."
Stanley said, nodding at the big computer in the middle of the room.
It took was still humming with power.

"They have their own fusion reactor!"
Nat exclaimed from further in the room.

"That's not good, it means that security is likely on its way."
Sly said.
"Right let's grab the data crystal and get out.
Nat, can you see a way of shutting that down?"

Gunther covered the door with his gun.
Sly and Stanley started taking the casing off of the server.

"Here it is," Nat said.
She grabbed the emergency handle and shutdown the reactor.

"Guys, I don't know what you did," Mickey's voice said over their radios.
"But these boxes just started making noises."

With the power off Sly and Stanley ripped the rest of the computer apart and took the crystal out.
There came a blood curdling scream from out in the corridor and Mickey's icon vanished from the group chat.

"Chris, I think we're going to need a quick exit."
Sly said.
The four of them edged to the door.
Nat took point, her sledgehammer being a close range weapon.
The others following behind.
There was a thump behind them and in the dark they made out an ill looking man.
His skin was covered in weeping sores and he wasn't moving entirely naturally.
Gunther shot him in the chest.
Initially the man barely reacted, two more shots saw him drop dead.

More of them rushed them from the way that they had come.
Nat wildly swung her sledgehammer into the first, while Stanley and Sly opened fire on its many friends.

The four of them made it back into the storage area.
Pretty Mickey lay on the floor.
There was very little blood.

"He's still alive!"
Nat said, checking his pulse.
"Argh!"
She exclaimed as Mickey bit her.
Stanley and Sly tried pushing him away.
From the feral look on his face Sly quickly deduced that it wasn't Mickey anymore and shot him.
Three shots to the head finally persuaded Mickey to release Nat and to die.

"Thanks, what was that?"
Nat asked.
Gunther crudely bandaged the big wound on her arm.
Then they continued their escape out of the doors.

The lights of the van flashed at them.
A couple of dead bodies were in front of it.

"Something I should know about?"
Chris asked, his hand gun sitting on the dashboard, as the crew piled in.

"Jerome may have left out some details about the job," Sly replied.
"Get us back to the hideout, the long way round, don't draw attention to us and try not to let anyone follow us."

"You got it boss," he said.
The van purred away, its electric motor silently speeding them along.

"I don't feel so good," Nat complained.
The skin on her arm where Mickey had bitten her looked really unhealthy.
Sores were developing up her arm.

"Take us to the doc, Chris," Sly said.

Dr Ahab was not best please to see them.
Nat was shaking and her arm looked even worse.

"Bloody hell Sly," Ahab said.
"What on Earth are you mixed up with?"
Sly had given him the precis of what had happened to Mickey and Nat.
Ahab ended up amputating Nat's entire arm, to cut the infection out, he'd said.
He'd worn some rather serious gloves and immediately incinerated the arm.
The scalpel, saw and gloves all went into the micro incinerator next.

"You had better hope that that was it.
The only known cure that continuously works on Inheritor symbiotes is injecting the blood from a Telosian.
And I can assure you that I don't have any of that.
The cure that works sporadically is nanites keyed to the infection."
Ahab said.
The computer was humming away on the sample he had taken.
He'd barely let it scan it before incinerating the blood sample, sanitising the scanner and incinerating the cloth.
The computer beeped and Ahab loaded an injector with the nanites it had produced.
He injected himself first, then each of them in turn and finally Nat got the shot.
"Hope you're feeling lucky Sly," he said.

"The crystal is empty," Chris said to Sly as they returned to the van.
Both of them had red marks from the injection.
Apparently the nanites would give them some immunity against the Inheritor infection.

"There's a surprise."
Sly said.
"Not sure we'll be seeing much of Jerome anymore."
