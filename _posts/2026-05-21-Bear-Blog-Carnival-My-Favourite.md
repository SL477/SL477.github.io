At the moment what I'm doing in in the Sevenoaks Slasher series [^1] is to expand out some of the world building to enable it to be a setting for other types of stories.
As I realise that a vicar solving small town crimes in the far future may be a little too niche.

I have the beginnings of a Lasers and Feelings game/run through, which hopefully I will one day get back to and complete.
And the first part of a Factorio play through, ok so as Factorio basically has no rules or lore about the only thing it has in common is that starts with a spaceship crashing[^3].
I originally started that one as an entry for the IndieWeb Carnival, but my leisurely writing pace saw that deadline go a long time ago.

In a somewhat similar vein I am having vague ideas of a kill team style event.
But before that I've got to do Gangs of Titan City, which to make a change I actually managed to write something for and it made the deadline!
