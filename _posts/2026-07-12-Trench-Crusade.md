New Antioch versus New Antioch (my noble vanilla New Antioch force (Albia), versus the perfidious Prussian force).

Here the scenario was relic retrieval.
In the central cross, surrounded by fog was a relic to capture.

### Turn 1

An uneventful turn, mostly involving the troops moving up into positions near the cross where the relic was located.

My sniper priest made an unsuccessful shot from his starting position at one of the Prussian Troops who showed his head.
And a sneaky Prussian infiltrator yeoman tried throwing a grenade at my lieutenant and missed.

My Heavy Mechanised Infantryman took up a position near the relic to behead anyone who came near him.

A grenade landed by my preacher, wounding him and killing the engineer by him.

## Turn 2

The Prussian Lieutenant charged into combat with the Albian Preacher.
A back and forth mêlée saw the Scotsman go down with a tank splitter sword to the chest.

Retaliation was immediate, with an Albian shock trooper firing back and wounding her.
Revenge spiraled as a Prussian threw a grenade at him.

The Albian Observer listened to the Voice of God and rather than climb up to the top of the trench by the relic, fell over.

To the Observer's left there was more fighting as the other Albian shock trooper, Yeomen pair and HMI took on the Prussian yeoman, cleric and Sniper Priest.
The sniper priest unsuccessfully shot at the shock trooper, who failed to kill the infiltrator, the cleric used his tank splitter sword to kill the HMI.
The yeomen failed to kill each other.
The Albian sniper priest shot at the Prussian sniper priest and gave him a minor flesh wound.

The Prussian flamethrower singes the two yeomen.

## Turn 3

The Albian Lieutenant edged closer to the relic killing the Prussian cleric on the way, ending up just out of reach as one of the Prussian soldiers grabs it and starts moving away.

The Albian Observer decided that sleeping in the cold mud was preferable to doing anything.

Another turn of indecisive fighting centered on the Prussian Lieutenant, who killed one of the Albian Yeomen near her and was knocked down by the other.
The shock trooper nearby and one of the Prussian Troops exchanged bullets and a grenade relatively painlessly.

The Albian Sniper Priest once again failed to kill his submachine totting opposite number on the Prussian side.

The Prussian Observer killed the Albian Lieutenant.
Pointing out the Prussian infiltrator yeoman, the infiltrator killed the shock trooper.
The two Albian yeomen took their revenge and killed him.

## Turn 4

The Prussian shock trooper on the right flank prepared for the passage of the relic by killing the nearby Albian shock trooper.

Then disaster for the Prussians, the Albian Observer finally got up and killed the Prussian Lieutenant.
Passing the batten onto the yeoman next to him.
The yeoman shot the shock trooper holding the relic, knocking him down and causing him to drop it.

The relic trooper picked himself up and dropped down into the trench to hobble away to safety.


Prussian victory, 10 victory points for holding the relic at the end.

## New Antioch versus Trench Pilgrims

Here the scenario was breakout.
The two sides need to cross the trench system and exit on their opponent's side.

## Turn 1

The two sides moved slowly towards each other.
The Albian Sniper Priest took a pot shot at the Pilgrim punt gunner, missing.
The punt gunner then killed the Albian Observer.

## Turn 2

The Albian Lieutenant rashly charged the two stigmatic nuns.
Succeeding in getting himself killed when the barely hit nun stabbed and shot him.

Nearby a trench pilgrim fumbled his shot against one of the shock troopers.
He was promptly injured by the two yeomen nearby.
That shock trooper then was injured by the punt gunner.
Who was lightly scratched by the sniper priest.

The war prophet healed the trench pilgrim and encouraged the two nuns to advance up onto the trench by them.

On the right the Heavy Mechanised Infantryman clambered down one trench and up onto the top of another.
When he was promptly killed by the shrine anchorite, which retreated.

The two posing yeomen nearby moved up to stand nearby to snap more selfies of each other for their Trench-Tok feed.
Enraged by this display the communicant failed to advance to meet them.
The engineer likewise failed to climb up the trench to join them.

A pilgrim sniper shot at the preacher.

## Turn 3

Killing the shock trooper by him, the war prophet charged towards the exit.
Selfie snapping away the posing pair on the right went for the trench sniper and killed him.
The punt gunner shot at the other posing pair after a brief advance.
The preacher advanced.

On the left the nuns killed the shock trooper near them.

In the centre the sniper priest took another pointless shot at the punt gunner.

Back on the right the shrine anchorite bludgeoned the preacher to death.
The engineer advanced and was promptly killed by the communicant.

The trench pilgrim killed one of the two yeomen.

## Turn 4

The less popular posing pair of yeomen took a couple of shots at the war prophet, knocking him to the floor.
He staggered to his feet and failed to escape the trenches.

Prancing through the shrine anchorite, communicant and a pilgrim.
The Trench-Tokker failed to escape the trenches, but got a good selfie.

On the left the faster nun escaped.
The other was gunned down by the sniper priest.

Trench pilgrim victory, 7 victory points for the Trench Pilgrims for the escape of the nun.

## Post game analysis

### Game 1

For the Prussians it was almost a perfect game.
With the only fumble of being unlucky that the Albian Yeomen were able to prevent the instant win of the relic going off the board.

For the forces of Albia, quantity over quality hurt them.
The Prussian force didn't have any massive brutes with them, but they could take a lot of hits and get into melee combat.
The forces of Albia also were too happy to get into meaningless fights and quickly bleed away their numbers.

### Match 2

Basically it was quantity over quality again.
The Albian forces could not go toe to toe with the pilgrims and had no answer to the shrine anchorite or communicant.

The nuns and war prophet also were able to effectively wander over the trenches and escape.
