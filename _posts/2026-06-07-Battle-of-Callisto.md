Vice Admiral Hasha Gonzalez stood in front of the vast screen window in the CIC of the starship *General Calista*.
She had after many years of command learnt to stop nervously pacing.
Today wasn't a day to be nervous of budget meetings or exercises, today looked to be the real deal.

An Inheritor warp tunneller had been detected by patrols on the outer edge of the system, it had been travelling for years to lay the warp lane.
As soon as it had detected the patrol ship it had flipped over and jumped back to where it had come from, making the return journey in a few seconds.

Nuclear mines had been dropped at the end of the warp route.
Experience said that that would not be enough.
If anyone survived the wave of Inheritor ships destined to appear shortly, then a massive counterattack would be launched against the system at the other end.
Gonzalez fumed that she didn't have the ships necessary to launch an attack now, but that would be a suicidal charge into the unknown.
Here at least the fleet might be forced to make a suicidal last stand, but the ring of Callisto (home to the fleet) at least meant that those who ejected could have somewhere to go.

"Contact Admiral," came the call of the sensor officer.
Two huge ships appeared on the holographic display.
They were broadly similar, asteroids having been turned into huge spaceships.
Fusion rockets pushed them through space and they were liberally coated in guns.
Dozens of escorts surrounded them, some appeared to be single biological creatures and all but one were clearly the results of biological manufacturing even if they were not creatures themselves.
The final escort was a human starship, the Federalist movement who wanted to properly bring back the old Federation (rather than the current fractious alliance) had taken to collaborating with the Inheritors.

"Fire on the lead rock ship."
Ordered Harsha.
The fleet, one cruiser, four destroyers, sixteen gun platforms, dozens of shuttles and hundreds of fighters, was everything that Callisto could put together (plus the guns mounted on the ring, many having been recently installed).
Courier ships had jumped away to summon reinforcements and another hundred or so picket ships from the scout forces had been recalled.

Missiles began traversing the vast distance between the two fleets on the display.
Harsha had them making regular movements to try to make life harder for the Inheritors.

Three hours after getting into detection range missiles started reaching their targets, or lack of them.
Both sides used munitions that had a degree of self-aiming, at the extreme opening ranges of fleet battles there were huge degrees of error in where the opposing fleet might be when the missile got to them.

Harsha saw the huge flash of light as one of her destroyers vanished.
An antimatter missile having found it. One of the Inheritor escorts vanished in its own burst of fire as a fusion missile detonated inside it.
Callisto was too poor to afford to stick antimatter into their missiles, what they had were a mixture of purely kinetic rounds, those which detonated their fusion rockets, high explosives and some ancient nuclear warheads (these mostly failed to detonate and the high explosives weren't powerful enough to breach the Inheritors' armour).

The two rock ships were aimed to swing in to drop their troops off on the ring, while their escorts swept away the Callisto Defence Fleet.

"Admiral, I'm detecting friendly contacts at our warp lane!"
The sensor officer yelled triumphantly, there were general cheers from the bridge crew.
Harsha outwardly smiled, but knew that the warp lane was several hours flight away and that assumed that this was more than a scouting force making sure that the Inheritors didn't park anything nasty there while the Federal Alliance assembled a fleet.

"That's good news lieutenant, now let's see if we can bag a few more Inheritors before they get here."
The admiral ordered.

"Sir, the President is ordering us to return to Callisto to destroy the rock ships."
Came the hesitant voice of the communications officer.
The cowardly self-serving orders from the President had got them into the position where the fleet had been separated from the ring.
Harsha was surprised that he hadn't fled on one of the evacuation ships, the two civilian starships in the system when the scouts had reported the Inheritor incursion had both been near fully loaded with civilians (although the grain and farming produce that were Callisto's main exports had not been offloaded).
The President had decided that the fleet was expensive and that it would be preferable for the Federal Alliance fleet to get blown up as they weren't his problem.
The issue with that was that the Federal Alliance had to respond to potential Inheritor attacks across twenty thousand light years of space and Callisto was on the outer edge of the trade routes.

"Very well," Harsha said.
"Helm take us past the rock with three drive units.
Tactical, if you would be kind enough to give that thing a broadside when get there.
And I'm countermanding the President's munitions rationing orders, there's little sense in saving the missiles for later."

The steady stream of shuttles heading to the warp lane had eased off as the ring's supply of shuttles ran out and the approaching Inheritor fleet made that a way of getting eaten sooner.
Without a warp drive all the shuttles could hopefully for was to be picked up by the incoming Federal fleet or chance upon a trader who hadn't heard that there was an Inheritor fleet on the other end of it.
It was incredibly dangerous, both for the passengers of the refugee fleet and for those on the other end of the warp lane.
As it was telling the Inheritors exactly where their next meal was and they wouldn't need to wait for a warp tunneller to create a new lane over the years of it flying slower than light.

The *General Calista* took a beating from the escorts as it blasted through them.
Its own surviving escorts hanging back to give covering fire.
The drone fighters proceeding it taking staggering losses as they cleared space of missiles and enemy fighters.
Most of the crews of the gun platforms had gallantly fought to the last man, at least until the President had ordered the fleet to back off.
Then the crews had started ejecting in earnest, the guns had some movement from steering jets but needed shuttles or tugs to properly move and no one was going to make that journey.
This meant that a load of fire directed towards the fleet came from the gun platforms that the Inheritors had boarded and captured (the gun platforms were also a cost saving exercise, little more than a big gun and a living space for a small crew they were a lot cheaper than a proper spaceship).

The *General* screamed in pain as one of the Inheritor escorts came alongside her.
The gunnery crew poured a full broadside into her and received one in turn.
A much more insidious threat was the boarding party the Inheritor ship dropped off before it backed away.
Hundreds of assault pods had been launched, hundreds intercepted by the point defence guns, but many dozens hitting the rear flank that the Inheritor ship had focused its fire on.

"Admiral, we have taken on boarders," announced the chief of security.
Who dropped back to her station to coordinate with the ship's many security teams.

"Keep them away from the gunnery decks and engines please Chief."
Harsha replied curtly, it wouldn't do to appear panicked.

A set of alarms went off that the petty officer on damage control silenced.
"Fires in the rear third of the starboard section, my guys are dealing with it."
He said sheepishly at her glance.
Fires on a spaceship were typically dealt with in two ways, either the fire suppression system/hoses extinguished it or you opened the airlocks and let a lack of atmosphere sort things out.
On any other day that would be either the Captain or Admiral's highest priority, today it wasn't.

"Gunnery, are we in range of the rock ship yet?" She asked.

"Not yet Ma'am. Only the forward missiles and we are running short on those.
The rail guns are ready again?"
The gunnery officer replied.

"Fire it.
In fact fire everything that can hit an Inheritor."
She replied.
The lights dimmed as the twin rail guns fired.
The nuclear warheads were in an iron shell and the magnetic rail ran for two kilometres through the ship.
Hitting ten percent of light speed (at least they would have several centuries ago when the ship was new and when it fired lighter antimatter charges) on their way and then detonating the shaped charges just before they hit the ship.
They made an impressive flash of light, but most of their energy was absorbed by the rockship's shields.

More of the Inheritor escorts took turns at coming at the *General*, it was a means of allowing each ship's shields time to recover and to prevent the human ship's shields from doing the same.
The human escorts were split between those who were willing to follow Harsha's orders to follow the cruiser into even more peril and those who preferred bravely sniping from a distance.

A huge explosion rocked the ship as a lucky hit took out one of the rail guns.
Trailing a cloud of debris the *General Calista* slammed into one of the rock ships. 

---

President Jerome Jackson watched the holographic display in horror as the *General Calista* vanished in a burst of interference.
The rock ship was still there when it cleared.

The attending Admiral and several Generals who had had a series of hopeful and triumphant expressions changed to match the President's expression.

"I want the Federation to send us the 13th Absolutionists, now."
The President decreed.
The Generals all looked uncomfortable at that.

"I'll get on it right away sir," an eager lieutenant said and hurried off.
Unfortunately the Absolution divisions were not the Federation's gift to give, they were part of the Anglican Church's Rose Order.
Divisions one through twelve were a mixture of convict troops and volunteers.
The thirteenth were more highly requested in anti-Inheritor operations than any other unit from any force (save fleets, as if they blasted the Inheritors out of space it meant that you didn't have to kill them on the ground).
The 13th Absolution Division were an all volunteer force of Telosian super soldiers, who had been adapted to fight Inheritors [^1].
Unfortunately being the most highly requested force meant that they were already fighting the Inheritors on San Pedro and were unlikely to be sent to Callisto any time soon[^2].

"Sir, I recommend evacuating all remaining civilians to the ring's catacombs and pulling all troops back to guard them."
Major General Francis Delaware chipped in.
He had been attempting to impose a sensible strategy on the defence.

"No! No retreat, we cannot sacrifice our land to the Inheritors."
President Jackson yelled.

"Sirs, incoming transmission from the Federation Alliance fleet."
Said one of the officers.

"Transmit coordinates of Inheritor warp lane now.
World Ender en route, if no response by the time it arrives it will deploy against the Callisto Ring."
Came the anonymous voice.

"Transmit the coordinates and request help here."
General Delaware snapped before the President could speak.

"Tell them I want the Thirteenth."
President Jackson said.
Obviously some survival instinct was overriding his natural stupidity.

The rockships reached the ring.
Drop pods hurled down onto the habitable inner surface of the Ring.
One of the rock ships moved to land on the inner surface, while the escorts and the other capital ship blasted away the anti-space guns on the ring.

---

Sergeant Tanner watched the drop pods falling in the distance.
He and his men were guarding the entrance to one of Callisto City's train stations.
Tanner was starting to worry about how the trains ran around the outside of the ring and that he might be facing Inheritors from above and below.
Their orders were to maintain order and guard the city's infrastructure, as the Inheritors were there to eat everyone and move on it was unlikely that they would care about the trains.

"Look lively lads," he said and flicked the safety off on his rifle.
A drop pod slammed into the tower block opposite them.
Glass and concrete fragments showered them and the street. There was the noise of screaming in the distance, civilians had been given conflicting advice about what to do.
More pods slammed to the ground all around them and soon they could see a mixture of the lizard man forms and raptors in the distance.

"They don't seem that interested in us at the moment sarge," one of the men exclaimed.
At that point the Inheritors must have heard him as a swarm of insects descended on the city.
The not well read manual on how to fight Inheritors advised troops to wear fully enclosed environment suits, preferably with armour and their own air supply.
Sergeant Tanner and his men were wearing flak jackets over their fatigues.
So they were a bit distracted by being bitten by the vicious variety of Inheritor insects to notice a squad of lizard men sneaking up on them.

"Ack," gurgled a private as he came to the unfortunate realisation that he was dead, a smoking hole straight through a gap in his armour.
Insects forgotten about, the squad and the rest of the platoon snapped around to fire at the aliens[^3] as they crept through the plaza outside the station.
Tanner let off a burst of laser fire at them, which when he did cause a hit was harmlessly absorbed by the chitinous armour.
He was distracted by a burst of awful screaming behind him.
Several men had been caught in the backwash of an acid grenade, it was hard to tell exactly how many.
He turned back and narrowly avoided being eaten by a raptor, killing it with a burst of fire.

"Fall back!" He ordered, realising that his men had followed his order already.
At least those in any state to do so.
He started moving into the station and was met by the Inheritors who had swept through from the star port below.

---
The Federal Alliance fleet arrived just over a week after the Inheritors had been detected at Callisto.
Which given its location was rather impressive, small comfort for the tens of millions who had died while the task force was assembled.

The Jovian Alliance had sent the task force's main battleship.
Two battle cruisers had come from the Papal Defence Fleet.
One corvette from the Anglican's Rose Order (the rest of the Rose Order's fleet was at San Pedro, along with an even bigger Catholic force).
And then fifty smaller ships from almost as many other places rounded out the rest of the task force.

The fleet aimed straight for the Callisto Ring, ignoring the pleas from the shuttles on the flight path to pick them up.
A small fleet of ships were jumping to and from the Callisto system and a selection of nearby bases belonging to members of the Federal Alliance, and they were doing a good job of rescuing people along the way.

Two other federal fleets were on their way.
One setup for a hostile invasion of the Callisto Ring and the other to jump down the Inheritor's warp lane to see what damage they could cause on the other side.

The first task force blitzed straight past the Callisto Ring without stopping.
In their flyby they had managed to blow apart the rock ship which had docked with the underside of the ring (not exactly a safe shot, but it helped thin the Inheritors' numbers for the ground troops) and all of the escorts still in space.
One of the federal ships had been destroyed and a couple of others badly damaged.

On their second, much slower pass, they dropped a selection of antimatter bombs on top of the landed rock ship.
With the battleship's particle cannon maintaining a constant power loss on its shield and the rest of the fleet using antimatter bombs in succession, they managed to bring down the rock ship.

Anyone surviving within a couple of thousand kilometres of the rock ship was now almost certainly deaf, if not much worse, two hundred medium yield antimatter explosions not being the most subtle weapon on a space habitat[^4].
But as they were relatively clean weapons, there wasn't any nuclear fallout to worry about (only some extraordinary violent winds from the blast waves).

The heavier ships, along with half of the escorts aimed themselves at the Inheritors' warp lane to join the third taskforce.
The other ships started a preliminary bombardment of the Ring, aiming at the worst Inheritor hotspots.

With no fanfare World Ender arrived. Similar in design to an Inheritor rock ship, in that it was an asteroid with guns and rocket engines.
It was equipped with a much bigger rail gun than most ships, this one was designed to kill all life on a planet in one shot and to destroy habitats.
It had a multinational crew, mostly because no one in the Federal Alliance trusted one another.

World Ender's attendant task force formed up around it as it glided towards the Inheritor warp lane.

Once they were clear of the regular warp lane, the counter invasion task force started to appear.
The troopships were escorted by a small flotilla of smaller ships, as it would be embarrassing for all of the troops to be killed on the way to the war-zone.

The 50th Abydosian Space Marine Division led the counter invasion.
They were shortly followed by the 7th Absolution Division of the Rose Order and 10th Valhallan Armoured Division.

Fighting was fierce until the main nesting sites were located and destroyed.
Then it was simply a moping up exercise.

The Hive world on the other side of the Inheritor's warp lane was destroyed in the apocalyptic space battle on the other side.
Then scouts swept the star system for other warp lanes, a thankless and dangerous task but infinitely preferable to the general massacres the Inheritors would inflict if left unchecked.

President Jerome Jackson, along with uncountable millions of his subjects, was never found and presumed eaten by the Inheritors.

[^1]: Strictly speaking they had been adapted to fight humans and had done that very well thank you very much. The Telosian Empire had once contained a few dozen star systems. Unfortunately making new Telosian super soldiers was a bit tricky, growth tanks being an unfortunate necessity. The failure rate was high and then not all of them developed the adaptions that enabled them to go toe to toe with the Inheritors. Which was why their empire days were long gone and people had given up on trying to get them to field more than a single under strength division against the Inheritors.

[^2]: If proactively offered the services of the 13th Absolution Division it indicated that either you were so strategically important that you had to have them or it meant that you had lost and the alternative was planet killer weaponry or higher.

[^3]: a subject of fierce and occasionally highly classified debate. To which many say if the answer is classified it means they aren't. Those who have survived fighting the Inheritors at close range mostly agree that they are a bio-weapon. Those who have survived boarding actions against Inheritor ships say that they know the answer and that it is classified; which most agree is unhelpful.

[^4]: the alternative would have been a near suicidal ground assault to get a city buster warhead to the other side of the ship's shields. Which as said forces who survived the attack would then find themselves in the blast zone meant that volunteers were hard to come by.
