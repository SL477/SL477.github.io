If I could have dinner with anyone in the world, I would be tempted to say Dan Abbnet, the author of the End and the Death and the Bequin trilogy.
Then I could try to get the answer of what happens in the last book Pandemonium out of him.
As apparently it was finished a couple of years ago, but strayed into territory that Games Workshop decided that didn't want to get into for a while.
Which makes me even more intrigued by it.

As I wasn't sure if the big reveal was going to turn out as big as Pariah made it out to be.
Or if it was just going to be something important to Bequin/Ravenor/Eisenhorn/where they were, but not the ground breaking Primarch releasing/new faction starting/Imperium civil war igniting/end times starting event that it was hyped up in the book for[^3].

## If you were to be stuck on a deserted island, what book would you bring with you?

On the dangerous assumption that this is some sort of holiday type thing and not an extreme survival situation[^1], then I think it would be Excession by Iain M Banks.

It is nearly infinitely re-readable on the grounds that I've read it four times and I'm still not sure I've got it all figured out.
