While not a great premise of rescuing Jabba the Hutt's son, the film makes up for it with a fantastic romp through the Clone Wars.

The Clone Wars were underutilized in the live-action movies, Episode 2 they start and Episode 3 they end.
Admittedly we do get hints of the Clone Wars expanse in episode 3, but that's about 5 minutes before they end.

This launches the Clone Wars TV series, which goes in depth into the Clone Wars (which isn't in chronological order). 

The vertical battle is a great scene.
It starts off with the Clone Troopers of the 501st dropping in from gunships into a jungle.
Then they scale a cliff while under fire from the Separatists.

In my mind there isn't enough foreshadowing that the friendly clone troops are going to be Imperial Stormtroopers.
And while the Separatists are not the Rebel Alliance, they are fighting the corrupt Republic which has shown its incompetence already.
But here, as in most of the episodes, the Separatists are moustachio twirling bad guys.
