A nice relaxing film with nice views of the countryside as the Orient Express works it's way from Istanbul to Paris.
Apart from the murder.

I prefer Kenneth Brannagh's version to David Suchet's.
David Suchet didn't appear to enjoy it nearly enough, while Kenneth loves it as much as the book version's Poirot.

The train is very luxurious and enjoyable, until it is stranded in the snow.
Then the murder is discovered.
A theme I do like in Agatha Christie books is things going a bit wrong and not according to plan.
This means that not everyone knows what is going on.
