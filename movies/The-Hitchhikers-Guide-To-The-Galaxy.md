A fantastic film.
As is traditional for Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy adaptions it is different to the book (I've read the book, listened to the radio series and seen the TV series).

Arthur Dent has a bad day when his house and planet are demolished (spoilers).
Luckily Ford Prefect (a joke that worked in the 1980s) is around to rescue him and takes him on an adventure into space.
To help Arthur settle into life as a galactic hitchhiker Ford gives him his copy of the Guide.

Zaphod Beeblebrox and Trillian join them, along with Marvin the Paranoid Android and their starship Heart of Gold.

We see a variety of planets and aliens, all explained by the guide and translated by the Babel fish.
