Disney had to somehow make one good Star Wars film and this was it.
The plot was so full of holes you could have flown an x-wing through it, but it looked amazing, had good performances by the actors and a load of banger lines.

`You're all rebels aren't you? I rebel.` - Jyn Erso, line in trailer cut from the movie after being deservedly mocked.

`Careful not to choke on your aspirations Director.` - Darth Vader.

Ok so I meanly quoted the two worst lines of the film, but it does honestly have a load of good lines in it.

The controversial bits were the deep fakes of Peter Cushing, aka Grand Moff Tarkin and Carrie Fisher, aka Princess Leia.
I think that they should have just allowed the two roles to be recast rather than CGI people in and let new actors take on the mantle.
There was a fair bit of that, with Gold Leader being archive footage from A New Hope.

Something I really didn't like about the film was the ending, with the fight scene going through the Rebel flagship and right to the doors of the Tantive IV.
As I feel it changes the tone of A New Hope.

Another criticism is the use of the Death Star.
I feel that they were trying to not take away from A New Hope by doing the 'single reactor ignition' trick to blast away fractions of planets, but I think they should have done that once rather than twice.

Once again Disney Star Wars has planets with a single settlement on it...
