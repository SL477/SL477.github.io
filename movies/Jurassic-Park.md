One of the first films that I remember seeing and still as good now as it was then.
We don't see much of the dinosaurs, but they're there and the special effects still hold up.

I'm not sure that I would want to go to a Michael Crickton designed theme park, after all he wrote West World too.

As a result of this movie my room did turn into a dinosaur museum.
But I'm not sure I would want one as a pet.
