The saga is complete.
The evil Empire is vanquished and peace and prosperity arrived in the Star Wars galaxy.

We see quite a few more ships in the Rebel Alliance fleet here than we have before.
We saw a hint of it at the end of Episode 5.

The speeder bike chase show the downsides of flying vehicles, every accident is a fatal one.

Jabba the Hutt gets his comeuppance, as he dies during the fight over the sarlacc pit.
Along with all of his henchmen.

Not sure the ewoks really worked as the fierce warrior culture, too much of the teddy bears picnic in them.

We have a brilliant space battle over Endor and a land battle on Endor.
Still have the criticism over the Star Wars trope of having a big battle no where near anyone.

We see the redemption of Darth Vader and the passing of the baton from Yoda to Luke.
