The final part of the lord of the rings trilogy.
As always I watched it in two halves, as otherwise the ten endings are even worse!

Having read the book I don't like the dues ex machina of the ghosts.
In the book they were used to surprise the corsairs and then the troops pinned by them in south Gondor were then free to go with Aragon to Minas Tirith.

The Scouring of the Shire I'm a bit more ambivalent about.
On the one hand it's a major payoff in the books and links back to stuff the hobbits saw on their way out of the Shire.
But on the other hand the movie would either need to be split in two or have it added on as a fourth film.
Plus I don't like the Shire being ruined.

We see a bit more of the lives of the orcs as Frodo and Sam journey across Mordor.
The Orcs are seen a bit more as victims in the books, but then you here a couple of orcs dreaming of being free to raid as brigands, so maybe not ideal citizens.

The charge of the Rohirrim is certainly one of the most epic scenes of cinematic history.
Not sure how realistic it is and afterwards there are a lot of horseless knights of Rohan in the western host.

Prince Imladris and the Knights of Dol Ameroth found themselves erased.
As did all of the Gondorian farmers, so it's unknown what the citizens of Minis Tirith were eating (lembas bread?).
Part of the unfortunate trope of cities in the middle of nowhere in fiction.
The farmers exist in the book!
As it's pre decent transport and refrigeration there should be a lot of farms around the place.

Unfortunately in the books the forces of Mordor get their food from hundreds of miles away from the shores of the lake of Rhûn and Isenguard from its annexation of the Shire (and prior Hobbit collaboration).
Using just roads, as Middle-Earth has no canals and there aren't decent sea routes, they would be using horse and carts.
There is only so far a horse and cart can transport supplies before the horse and driver have eaten all of the supplies before they reach their destination (and there is a reason why supplies went by water if possible).
