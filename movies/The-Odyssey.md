A pretty faithful adaptation of Homer's epic.
And something of a spiritual sequel to Troy[^1].

Spiderman (Odysseus junior) and Lady Penelope are held captive in Ithaca by suitors wanting to marry Lady Penelope.
Odysseus left 20 years ago to sack Troy and hasn't come back.
They have had word of the victory at Troy, as the other kings of Greece have reappeared.

I'm assuming that this predates proper navigation, as his method of navigation appears to be picking a random direction and sailing that way and hoping[^2].
His other problem is that it also predates refrigeration, so they loot every island on the way.
This other problem causes a whole load of other issues as Odysseus crashes on every cursed and enchanted island in the sea.

In the film Odysseus says how his crew think he is cursed by Poseidon and the other god's for breaking Zeus's Law.
In the book he is more explicitly cursed by Poseidon for destroying his favourite city and offering his symbol of a horse to Athena.
Then for blinding his son the Cyclops.

To demonstrate that the Greeks could navigate Spiderman takes a ship to Sparta to chat with Menalaus to see if he knows what has happened to Odysseus.
And at least the sea journey is uneventful.

[^1]: Which was not a very faithful adaptation of Homer's Iliad.

[^2]: Which makes you wonder how they got to Troy in the first place.
