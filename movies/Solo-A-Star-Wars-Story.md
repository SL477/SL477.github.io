There are a few good visuals, such as the Maw chase, but overall it's not a great film.
About the only good thing it did was spare us from a Kenobi movie (I saw the first episode of the series Disney forced upon us).

In the opening crawl when it said Hyper Fuel I knew it was going to be bad and it was.

I didn't like the Empire effectively being subordinate to a load of random criminal gangs.
And the Rebel Alliance being a response to that?

It brought Darth Maul back to the cinematic universe.
I know he came back in the TV Series, but I felt that when Sidious crushed him that should have been it for him.

The lack of a Wookie Life Debt was a good change.
I always thought it was a terrible addition to the legends universe.
