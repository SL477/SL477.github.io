One of the best films ever.
And one of the best books ever. 

Having been to New Zealand (a while ago now) it's a bit strange recognising some of the places that I went to.

Tolkien's world building is fantastic and the film barely does it justice[^1].
I have always preferred the first film to the next two, it's more of an honest adventure story than anything else and you can see why DnD stole so heavily from Middle-Earth.

I do like the sparing use of CGI in the film, when they made the Hobbit everything could be a special effect and the films suffered as a result.
But over 20 years later LOTR still looks great.

[^1]: I fully support getting rid of Tom Bombadill. But the cities with nothing around them are what I have more of a problem with.
