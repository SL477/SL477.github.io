Daniel Craig's last last outing as James Bond.
The music throughout[^1] calls back to On Her Majesty's Secret Service, my favourite James Bond film, they also visit a few of the locations and drive the car from that film.

Rami Malek plays the sinister Dr No (Lyutsifer Safin), a repeat of the villain from the very second James Bond (the first from MGM, if you count the very terrible first Casino Royal (not the one with Daniel Craig)).
I'm guessing that this means that Rami Malek won't be the next Bond, which is a pity because he is very good.

The sprawling fight in Cuba was a definite highlight of the film as Bond fights Spectre, 007 and the Cuban police.
All while freelancing for the CIA.

Another deafening highlight was the opening fight in Italy, using the classic Aston Martin DB5.
The DB5 also steals the show as one of the best Bond cars, so good it has been in a few Bonds.

Ben Whishaw returns as the long suffering Q.
You may recognise his voice as Paddington and Wallace.
He gives Bond a few good toys but, as is the way with the more serious Daniel Craig Bonds, there isn't Bond's normal comic rampage through Q branch.

Felix Lighter appears in all the best James Bonds and this is no exception.
He is Bond's best friend, as shown in Licence to Kill when Bond is his best man.

Ernst Stavro Blofeld makes something of a cameo appearance, he was previously captured by Bond and put in Magneto's prison cell.
There are a lot of callbacks to Spectre and Casino Royal in this movie.
Prior to the Daniel Craig era each James Bond movie was effectively standalone.
