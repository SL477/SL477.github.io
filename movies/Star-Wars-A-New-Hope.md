It still stands up almost fifty years after it was made, mostly because George Lucas carried on touching it up.
But I'm not going to nitpick on perfection.

The definitive science fiction film.
Spawner of a thousand books, games and TV series.

Critics of the prequel series politics seem to have forgotten the boardroom scene in the Death Star.
Where Tarkin's senior staff discuss the end of the Imperial Senate.
Although we aren't given much idea about the politics of what's going on, but it's more show don't tell.
We see that the stormtroopers have killed Luke's aunt and uncle, we don't know exactly what the political reasons are.

Every spaceship is cool, we don't see as much of the Death Star in action as we do in Rogue One.
The X-Wing has to be the best ship.

As the lightsabers were made out of metal the lightsaber fights are a bit more stilted than in the prequels.

The final Death Star battle is something of a masterpiece.
But even things like the Millennium Falcon docking in the Death Star are made incredible.
