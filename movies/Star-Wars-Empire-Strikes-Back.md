What more to say, one of the best films ever.

We have the dramatic Battle of Hoth near the start, after some fantastic establishing of the planet as a setting.
We see Blizzard Force and their specific snow adapted armour, along with the Rebel's winter troops.
Something that other sci-fi settings seem to struggle with is that each planet is going to have its own climate/gravity/etc and what works on one may not work on another.

Lando Calarissian has to be one of the best minor characters in anything.
Bespin also has a good time as being established as another unique planet.
A floating city above a gas giant and another glimpse at seeing civilians in Star Wars.

By contrast Degobah is a rather generic jungle world, but I guess that that was significantly cheaper than trying to come up with a more unique world.

We see how brutal promotion is in the Empire, as Captain Piett becomes Admiral as the former Admiral Ozzel is Force choked by Vader.
But I suppose serving on the Executor kind of reveals that you're on the side of the bad guys.[^1]

`The Force is with you young Skywalker. But you are not a Jedi yet.` - Darth Vader.

We get a good Lightsaber fight between Luke and Vader.
I liked the bit where Vader is hurling everything at Luke while battering him with a lightsaber.
It's basically what the Darth Bane book said lightsaber fights should be, using memorised sword fighting techniques while using the Force as the primary weapon.

Plus the rather extended chase of the Millennium Falcon.
Twirling through asteroids, being chased by Tie Fighters and Star Destroyers.
With the Executor as the best Imperial ship that we see.

[^1]: the end when it focuses on Piett's face is brilliant.
