`Now this is pod-racing!` - Anakin Skywalker.

I forgot how good this film looked, the Trade Federation blockade of Naboo looked fantastic. 

As does Naboo itself, it mostly seems a clone of Earth apart from the water core and all of the sea monsters.
Theed and Ooch Gunga were fairly different from each other, one a picturesque city that wouldn't look too out of place in Europe. And the other the city under the sea.

Darth Maul gets to menace everyone, until he's tragically cut down.
In quite a good Lightsaber duel.
Not sure about his double bladed lightsaber though. 

Pod Racing is brilliant, I loved the Star Wars Episode 1 Racer game where you got to race on a load of different planets in the outer rim.

The traditional battle has a good Star Wars twist, the Gunguns appear to be using shields, spears and catapults (naturally more high tech).
Against the droids of the Trade Federation who have the near disposable battle droids.

The space battle was fairly run of the mill, it called back to the end of Episode 4, as did the triumph through Theed.
