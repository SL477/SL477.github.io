This was a very mixed film.
There were bits that were brilliant and bits that were exceedingly dull for a long time.
It did not help that there wasn't a coherent story, not because it was all in French, it seemed to be random parts of De Gaulle and the resistance's history.
From just before the fall of France to the liberation of Algeria.

The best part was about the North Africa campaign.
The worst was about the Tom Holland lookalike and his occasional forays into resistance[^1].

It shows a bit of the politics of Vichy France and their attempts for a third way of neutrality.
Which appeared code for complete collaboration with the Nazis.

[^1]: as Max Hastings notes, the French Resistance mostly came into being 5 minutes prior to Allied soldiers turning up.
