This has to be the best opening of any film.
The huge panoramic view of the Battle of Coruscant.
Thousands of starships blasting at each other, and more Starfighters shooting at each other.

The films main flaw is that it is based around Anakin Skywalker's massively out of character fall to the dark side.
Apart from his flashes of anger at the Jedi council and, basically ordered, execution of Count Dooku (which I'm not really sure counts against him); plus massacre of sand people in the last movie (mostly off screen), there was little foreshadowing.
Then Anakin is all in, killing the Jedi who have been his family for the last ten years and trying to kill his adopted brother/father/mentor/boss Obi-wan Kenobi.

`So this is how liberty dies, with thunderous applause.` Padmé Amidala.

The film does have the occasional good line, despite George Lucas being terrible at dialogue (he missed his calling at making silent movies).

The Clone Wars TV series also does a fairly atrocious job at foreshadowing Anakin's fall.
There was his brief fall to the dark side in the arc on the weird planet with father, brother and sister, but Anakin, Obi-wan and Asoka have their memories erased.
He also tortures Poggle The Lesser, with the Jedi next door who didn't notice.

In the Legends book Rogue Planet (I think) Luke discovers that there was an incident on the planet where Anakin ended up killing a creature using the Force, while he was a padawan in front of Obi-wan.
Luke sees that as a clue that Kenobi should have picked up on.

I feel that Episode 1 should have been cut, or moved to be an older Anakin in more of a protagonist role.
Or in the Clone Wars series have Anakin using dark side powers when the Jedi aren't around.
We are told that Anakin confused Asoka's training by teaching her dark side adjacent stuff, but that's a single line in a hundred episodes.

Padmé's death is also idiotic and I'm taking at least one star off of it for having such a stupid scene in it.
Not quite as idiotic as everything between 1 second into Episode 9 and the closing credits (which has to have a rating of -1 million, it being pre AI means that that was human stupidity and they actually had a decent script for the movie and chose the other one!).
