In the Slasher universe the main currency, or at least the main one I deal with, is the dollar.
Initially I just gave it that name because I was on a hurry when writing and wanted it to be a bit more grounded than just picking one of the many defunct currencies and I didn't want them to be called credits.
Plus while governments and companies might be happy to do bartering it's a bit inconvenient in a story.

## What’s depicted on your coinage and banknotes and what are the stories behind those depictions?

Empress Carla Ludendorff I is the current monarch to grace the physical coins and banknotes of Earth's dollars.
With banknotes depicting Earth and Luna to show the extent of her domain.
And other symbols to show that Earth is part of the Federal Alliance.

## Tell me about the money of your world, and the structure of the economy

Earth is part of the Sol system and its colony worlds[^1].
The trades between these planets and space stations are either done in dollars or via bartering.

Longer distance trades are done through Federal Credits, the old currency of the Federation, a purely virtual currency.

The economy is broadly similar to our own capitalist economy.
But with no instantaneous communications between planets and star systems, to an extent it is similar to the economy back in the age of sail/the pre telegraph era.
There might be galaxy spanning mega-corporations, but to an extent they are more networks of subsidiaries working together (think some of our world's global law firms). 

Each world[^2] might have its own internal economy, laws and so on.
Some are more or less self sufficient and some aren't.

Star systems are connected by warp lanes.
These channels scattered with exotic matter allow ships to travel practically instantaneously between the stars.
Although as there are typically toll collectors at the other end[^3], many drop to sub-light speeds early and try to avoid paying.

Manufacturies can build most things easily.
Think a regular factory crossed with a 3D printer.
Some things require specialist manufacturing facilities: antimatter, warp engines.
And of course someone needs to collect the raw materials.

For worlds inside the Federal Alliance there is a system of galactic law and general stability (there have on occasion been wars within the alliance).
Some worlds outside the Alliance try to be associate members.
Others like the Ruusan Republic are corrupt lawless hellholes.

Outside the boundaries of Known Space is who knows what.
There are scattered human colonies and the remains of some.
Unknown dangers, rogue robots and the Inheritors.
Those provide loot and/or trading opportunities for daring space travellers, whilst others plow the more established trade routes.

[^1]: there are no sentient aliens to clear things up

[^2]: Planet/moon/space station/habitat etc. big thing with people in or on it.

[^3]: Someone has to keep dropping exotic matter off on the lane every so often and that has to happen slower than light.
