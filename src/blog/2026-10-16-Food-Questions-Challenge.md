## What was your favourite food growing up?

At my childhood home there was an apple tree.
So I had a lot of apples growing up.
Especially in the week or so when they are ripe, then within about an hour they seemed to be rotten and full of wasps.

The millionaires shortbread at the local bookshop was also brilliant.

## What is your favourite food that is made locally? (In your region/country.)

The Kottu rotti at Croydon Boxpark was brilliant, I don't know why they shut down but I suspect it was due to the high prices there.

In a similar vein the Guyanese place there was also brilliant.
Somehow that place in Boxpark shutdown, despite selling food as fast as it was made.

Roast dinners are also brilliant.

## What is your favourite food generally?

Chicken Tikka Masala, it may not be authentically Indian.
But it's brilliant.

The croissants they make at Lidl (other supermarkets are available) are also amazing.
Mysteriously all the cafes are selling suspiciously similar croissants (for 4 times the price).

## What is one food you have had in the last year that you remember warmly?

I have had a lot of Thai Massaman curry in the last year.

I worked my through a load of Mexican dishes.

## What food do you really want to try?

After seeing an advert ten seconds ago, Terry's white chocolate orange.

I would like to try making a Kottu rotti of my own at some point, as now I don't live in Croydon it's even more of a pain to get to the Sri Lankan shop.

## Do you take photos of your meals?

Only very occasionally.

## Savoury or sweet?

Sweet definitely.

## Spicy or no?

Definitely spicy.
I have no intention of taking the sort of military grade spices from challenges.
Particularly after trying a tiny speck of some two million scoville chilli and having it burn my tongue for ages.

## Tea or coffee? (Both? Neither?)

Neither. I've never managed to like either of them.
