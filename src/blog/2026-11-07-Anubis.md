Ugo sat in his control throne in geostationary orbit above the desert world of Anubis.
The airless ochre dust could be considered beautiful, but few people saw it in person and most from orbit.

Anubis was a starship factory.
Was being the operative word.
Since the Fall the supply of warp engines had gone to zero.
Which had been something of a disaster for Anubis.
Once their precious supply of warp drives were used there had been no more for a while.
As a backstop Anubis had been building sub-light starships.

Today was a special day, a delivery of warp drives, a small fleet of ships had dropped into the star system and were carrying enough for fifteen new starships.
Ugo had barely believed it, four hundred years had gone by and Anubis had constructed appropriately one ship per decade.
Their pre-Fall output had been closer to a ship per month.
And with their slower than light ships had more or less kept up that tempo.
Rumour had it was that there had been a huge border skirmish between the Federal Alliance and the Ruusan Republic.
Other rumours were that the warp drive factory had been rediscovered or that they had been seized from pirates.
Marcus, the lead designer, had surmised that the pirate theory was more likely.
What with the rather eclectic mixture of drives they had been told to accept.

An average warp drive was a metal cylinder three hundred metres long and fifty metres wide.
Each weighed significantly more than if it were built entirely of solid matter.
Which was why most starships were significantly bigger than that, the sub-light starships Anubis had been producing were often smaller than the FTL ones.
With settled space spanning a sphere approximately twenty thousand light years across (with huge unexplored tracts inside it) sub-light travel was only really an option in a few areas. 
The path finder ships that laid warp lanes (and maintenance ships) having been customers well before the Fall.
Although hobbyists, eccentrics, explorers and the military had always been minor customers.
With only a finite number of warp drives in the galaxy and the knowledge that accidents, piracy and war meant that year on year there were fewer warp drives around; shipping companies and governments had started purchasing sub-light ships as a form of insurance.

The Autumn's Sorrow was a heavy cargo carrier.
At twenty kilometres long and five across, she wasn't the biggest ship that Ugo had seen.
Anubis had built, and indeed was building, bigger ships.
But she was indeed bigger than the average flying mountain of a starship.
Her bulk emphasized by the smallness of the ships in the escorting fleet, which were often not much bigger than their warp drives (if indeed they even carried them, military vessels being more likely to be coming under attack than most ships).

The first of the warp drives was soon transferred to one of the factory stations.
Ugo normally did not like ships flying through his factory that were not under the full control of the Anubis Space Traffic Control.
But with something as valuable as a warp drive, he was glad that no one could blame him or the Anubis factory for anything going wrong.

During the construction of this new fleet of starships he took as many opportunities as he could to visit the Autumn's Sorrow and see its vast store of warp drives.
Many of these were undergoing self-repair, with some very esoteric materials being delivered to the ship to accommodate this.
One of them had required a flask of exotic matter, which had required a starship to make a round trip of over five thousand light years to obtain it and bring it back.
The nearby battery moon[^1] of Osiris had delivered vast stocks of antimatter to the ship to power the self repair.

The first ships to appear out of the vast space docks were the cruisers and battle cruisers.
Not too much bigger than their warp drives and effectively just flying space cannons, they were something of a staple of Anubis.
After a little shakedown jaunt through the system, they were moved to one of the finishing stations to to get their crew quarters sorted out and any remaining kinks repaired.

One of Ugo's favourite projects was the vast carrier ship they were building.
A huge chunk of hyper diamond had been formed into a ten kilometre long behemoth to haul smaller ships around.
Plus with its onboard factories (when finished) it would be capable of building more ships on its way.

But his baby was his own ship.
At barely a hundred metres long it was an insignificant speck compared to the space mountains Ugo usually built.
But it was his insignificant speck.
The *Occasional Tourist* had taken him on a few leisurely cruises around the Anubis system.
A habitation ring provided him with half a gee of gravity, which could be spun up to one gee if he was prepared for some motion sickness.
The system could be retracted into the main body of the ship, so that he could use the ship's fusion rocket to provide one gee of acceleration.
Ugo had initially liked the idea of using a solar sail for propulsion, but then he wouldn't be able to get anywhere and back before being missed at the shipyard.
He dreamed occasionally of retiring and flying off into interstellar space in the Tourist.
It had a stasis pod, so he need not worry of starving to death in interstellar space.
Although with its fusion rocket it would take a decade to get to the next star system.
He and the Tourist had flown to most of the major planets in the Anubis system.
The not quite a brown dwarf gas giant on the outer edge of the system was one he had been accruing extra holiday days for.
The innermost planetoid would require a more specialist craft to brave the harsher radiation of the star and Ugo had reluctantly decided that it wasn't worth the effort.
As Marcus and others on his team had pointed out, most of his excursions were to the habitats in the L5 Lagrange point.
Or just doing a grand orbit of Anubis itself.
But as Ugo sipped from his cup of Earl Grey tea in his captain's chair on the bridge (and main living area) he could imagine himself as a grand starship captain off to explore the universe.
"Computer, second star to the right and straight on till morning. Engage."

[^1]: an array of solar sails and panels on the moon sent power to a factory where it was converted into equal amounts of matter and antimatter. Not what one could describe as an efficient process, but with the effectively limitless power output of a star it mattered little.
