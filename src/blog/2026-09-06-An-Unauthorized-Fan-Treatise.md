This was a very weird book, that for some reason I couldn't put down.
It's in the style of a true crime exposé crossed with a website.
In some of the chapters the footnotes are longer than the main text.
Some chapters are all comments. The story ends on something of a cliff hanger, but I'm ok with that.

It reminded me a bit of the fandom shipping Sherlock and Watson in Sherlock (to the point where it is nodded to in the episodes).
