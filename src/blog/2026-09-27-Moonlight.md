High above the gas giant Apollo there was a bright flash of light and the giant starship Pandora's Heart appeared.
The ship's prow was reminiscent of the head of a hammerhead shark, but one four hundred metres across.
The rest of the ship was equally shark-like, the secondary observation deck in the dorsal fin (the primary in the hammerhead).
Other fins had found uses as docking ports.
The ship's designers had clearly given up on the tail, twin fusion rockets and an antimatter drive helped power the three kilometres of ship through the void.
The colossal warp engine was hidden in the centre of the rear quarter of the ship, away from most of the crew, but not too close to the sublight drives.

Joseph Isaacs stood near the front of the primary observation deck of the Pandora's Heart. 
Huge windows[^1] showed the star system Cassiopeia.

Cupid was currently a blindingly bright source of light, it was mostly made up of ice.
But curiously also a thin coating of diamonds.
It was currently in the full glare of the local star, but as Joseph watched the moon was shaded by the Apollo Habitat.
The giant ring was comfortably several times the size of the moon.
The Apollo gas giant was a mixture of dirty brown and red bands of gas.

Joseph was there as one of the representative of the Cassiopeia Reconstruction Initiative.
The company had been setup by Exelsius Corporation, a company which dreamed of one day being as galaxy spanning as Collective Industries or the Jovian Consortium.
A few passengers had arrived early like him.
The Pandora's Heart was a giant cruise ship, its legendary windows perfect for the coming spectacle.

"If you look to the right, you will see the remains of a star whale," said the voice of the woman the ship had decided was needed as tour guide.
Who was on the central podium. Joseph looked to the right as instructed and saw the huge remains.
At a mere two kilometres long the star whale had been a mere escort to the Inheritor fleet here.
A tug was pushing the remains further away from the warp lane.

"The flashes you see from Apollo are the detonations of antimatter bombs."
The tour guide said, possibly a bit too casually.
"The containment forces cannot let any of the Inheritor creatures leave, or they could infect another star system."

To either flank of the cruise liner was a destroyer in the Exelsius Corporation livery.
At only a few kilometres away they were almost criminally close to the ship, but they were there as psychological support for the honoured passengers.
Three other destroyers, near invisible but to the keen eyed, were there for actual protection.
Although the several hundred warships of the Federal Alliance fleet busy fighting the Inheritors still growing in the gas giant were unlikely to let anything get through.

Bishop Susannah Renard was announced as she entered.
She was there on behalf of the Anglican Church, whose Rose Order had made up a majority of the ground forces on the Apollo Habitat (at least a majority of those who weren't part of the Apollo Civil Defence forces).
Most of the Rose Order had left now, the genetically engineered troops of the 13th Absolution Division, whose mere presence scoured the biological taint of Inheritor contagions from people being unsuited to being near civilians.
The other Absolution Divisions, made up of regular humans, had also been sent back to St. Michael's Retreat.
What was left were their combat engineers to retrieve their heavy equipment and a few of their escort ships.

Cardinal Angelou of the Papal States, was announced next.
Joseph had the cruel suspicion that he had been lurking nearby so that people would know that the Vatican had sent someone more important than the Anglicans.
Twenty of their ships remained in system, equal to the entire fleet that the Rose Order had deployed.
He also had a bigger entourage of aides and bodyguards than the Bishop.

A transport ship, formally a cargo liner, passed them going in the other direction.
Some of the refugees of Apollo. Those who had been through decontamination.

Pre war the Cassiopeia system had boasted a population just shy of a billion, it had also been something of a tourist trap, despite being on the edge of known space.
This had meant that it had received a prompt response to its cries for assistance.
Despite that near three hundred million people were amongst the dead and missing[^2].

A pair of space stations orbited near the habitat.
In complete lack of comfort they were holding most of the remaining population of Apollo.
One was used for those yet to go through decontamination and the other was for those post decontamination waiting to discover their destiny.

"To your left you can see World Ender."
Announced the tour guide.
There were a few gasps from the dignitaries of the Federal Alliance who were witnessing all of this.
Joseph took a sip of the champagne he had collected from a passing waiter.
Instantly regretting it.
World Ender was a giant ancient spaceship[^3], currently in the shape of a giant ring and fully visible behind one of Apollo's moons.
"Do not worry ladies and gentlemen, we are well outside of its range."
The moon passed through World Ender, a huge blast of light scouring the surface and the radiation penetrating through the crust to kill any life on or in the moon.

"Even World Ender cannot hope to do the same for Apollo and the gas giant would render its anathema field ineffective.
For that Dr Bradshaw and her team have developed a different solution."
A woman who presumably was Dr Bradshaw stepped up to the podium.
Joseph tuned out as Dr Bradshaw started explaining in detail her solution to the problem.
Joseph had clocked that a lot of the aides were suddenly paying rapt attention, presumably the ones involved in military intelligence.
The Federal Alliance was by no means a unified force, it being most of the states formally in the Galactic Federation.
Joseph took the time to do a bit of schmoozing in the crowd.

He watched as World Ender seemed to split up and expand.
The Apollo Habitat's steering engines had oriented itself to be edge on to World Ender and to hold itself still.
A ten thousand kilometre wide habitat ring was replaceable.
The anathema field generators on World Ender were irreplaceable.
The blast this time was much longer.
The two space stations containing most of the world's population were on the edge of the effect. But that would only help speed up their trips through decontamination.

The civilians on the space stations would not be going home anytime soon.
The risk of remaining contaminates was too great.
The Cassiopeia Reconstruction Initiative would be stripping away all of the air, soil and water and taking the habitat down to its base material first.
Then remake the biosphere again.

"And now is the time we are all here for."
The tour guide announced.
The lights dimmed and several small orbital structures were enhanced on screen.
An overlay option appeared in Joseph's neural implants of the view from the command ship (which only had a few VIPs, in considerably less luxury).
Here were a lot of technicians and a few screens, whose display's were not facing the camera, were watching the planet from a much closer position.

"All systems green, Admiral," came the voice of one of the controllers.

"Excellent. Commander you may fire when ready." Came the voice of Admiral Jakarta.

"On my mark," said another voice.
Two of the technicians had put physical keys into two ominously red buttons.

"Fire!" Yelled the voice. The keys were turned and buttons pressed.

Out of the windows nothing obviously happened.
There had been a few flashes from Apollo a few minutes ago as antimatter warheads had been dropped. And flashes as the containment fleet came under fire.

"It's started," one of the crowd exclaimed.
The storms on the gas giant suddenly seemed to go into overdrive.
Parts of the planet bulged out at weird angles.
Then it started to more noticeably shrink.
The moon Cupid once again emerged from behind the planet to dazzle the watching crowds.
Many of whom started trickling out of the observation deck, the singularity cannon had fired and it would be many hours yet before Apollo fully vanished.

Joseph watched Cupid pass through World Ender.
The observation deck's window screens dimming and filtering out the blinding flash as the diamonds scattered the light of the anathema beam across space (the high intensity gamma radiation and quantum effect apparently wasn't deflected).

He took his leave of the proceedings to return to his cabin.
Several starships drifted by the Pandora's Heart, which he saw, doubtless part of the continuing evacuation.
The Cassiopeia Reconstruction Initiative would be borrowing the two space stations once they were free of people, at least until another world was attacked by the Inheritors, so it was unlikely to be any ships belonging to them.
He was interested in the hulk of a mostly destroyed cruiser as it was towed towards the warp lane.

In the morning Joseph took one of the ship's trains back to the observation room.
Now the gas giant was more the size of a regular planet.
Whether it would completely vanish into the singularity was unknown.
The military only cared that the atmosphere was gone and the Inheritors either sucked into the black hole at the centre or crushed in the metallic hydrogen sea.

It was unknown whether the moons would survive.
The equivalent of Earth's mass had been dropped into Apollo, in the form of its new black hole and it could mean that the moons would find new orbits, be ejected from the star system or collide with Apollo.
Joseph hoped that the company would make sure that they were saved.

[^1]: they were a spin off of active camouflage, a four hundred metre wide window being incredibly impractical on a spaceship.

[^2]: the Inheritors ate people, so missing generally meant dead.

[^3]: as the technology for making new warp engines was lost (officially), every ship in the star system was at least four hundred years old. World Ender was over a thousand years old, old before building new ships became somewhat problematic.
