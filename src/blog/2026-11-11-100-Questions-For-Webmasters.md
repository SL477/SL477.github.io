<dl>
<dt>1. Please introduce yourself.</dt>
<dd>My name is Tom and I'm a software developer.</dd>

<dt>2. How long have you been making websites?</dt>
<dd>From about 2017-18 my personal site has existed in some shape or form. I did learn some HTML and CSS in secondary school.</dd>

<dt>3. And what got you into the hobby?</dt>
<dd>In 2017ish I was employed as a software developer for a very specific software framework and decided to branch out to learn more about more programming. Eventually this turned into tinkering with the website for its own sake.</dd>

<dt>4. What kind of website are you most interested in?</dt>
<dd></dd>

<dt>5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?</dt>
<dd>you come up with the design as you go along?
I came up with the design as I went along. Usually things start out in Joplin and then make their way to the site.</dd>

<dt>6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.</dt>
<dd>Google.</dd>

<dt>7. What's your favourite part about making websites?</dt>
<dd>Things appearing on the computer relatively quickly and not needing to be gated by app-stores or the like.</dd>

<dt>8. And the thing you struggle with the most?</dt>
<dd>Design.</dd>

<dt>9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?</dt>
<dd>Mostly, that's why I let the static site generator generally let it deal with it.</dd>

<dt>10. How confident are you with CSS?</dt>
<dd>Fairly confident when I have an idea of what to aim for.</dd>

<dt>11. Do you know how to correctly use &lt;dl&gt;?</dt>
<dd>I've used them about once.</dd>

<dt>12. What is your favourite HTML element?</dt>
<dd>Div.</dd>

<dt>13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?</dt>
<dd>If completely from scratch, then try to remember the command that sets up a blank webpage in VSCodium. If a new page on my site, then import the main template and go from there.</dd>

<dt>14. Do you know JavaScript?</dt>
<dd>Yes, at work I use VB.Net so that's my best language. But JavaScript is not that far behind now.</dd>

<dt>15. How about PHP?</dt>
<dd>I have made a PHP site before. At some point I have a desire to fire up the Pi again and have that do some things in PHP for me.</dd>

<dt>16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?</dt>
<dd>Yes and more or less. Having an SSG means that it's a conscious decision to break with design.</dd>

<dt>17. Are you more focused on content or design?</dt>
<dd>Generally content. Although the number of redesigns the NavBar has received shows that I'm not afraid to tinker.</dd>

<dt>18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?</dt>
<dd>Yes, two of them and the main one has a fair few subdomains.</dd>

<dt>19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?</dt>
<dd>It rather depends on what they are doing.</dd>

<dt>20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?</dt>
<dd>Yes. I object to the HTML validator saying my site is wrong. They are wrong! All tags must be closed, I do not care what the standard says. Sometimes I check on it and sometimes I actually on its suggestions</dd>

<dt>21. What are your opinion on buttons and banners?</dt>
<dd>At some point I need to create a proper button banner for this site.</dd>

<dt>22. What do you think of button walls in particular?</dt>
<dd>Meh.</dd>

<dt>23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?</dt>
<dd>Given that my second site looks remarkably similar to the main one, I'm going to say that it would probably be about the same.</dd>

<dt>24. Are you envious of other people's websites?</dt>
<dd>Yes. I would like to make one of those sites that emulate Windows, but I would never find the time to do so. Somewhere on the site is the terminal emulator, which took an obscene amount of time and only does a limited number of Linux commands.</dd>

<dt>25. What text editor do you use?</dt>
<dd>VSCodium</dd>

<dt>26. Why do you use that one?</dt>
<dd></dd>

<dt>27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?</dt>
<dd>Because it does what VSCode can do without the spyware.</dd>

<dt>28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?</dt>
<dd>Never heard of it.</dd>

<dt>29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?</dt>
<dd>Somewhere around 100-500 mb. At some point I need to let Astro do its thing to properly compress all of the images.</dd>

<dt>30. Do you keep local backups of your files?</dt>
<dd>The magic of Git means I do.</dd>

<dt>31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?</dt>
<dd>Generally simple, but it depends on what it's for.</dd>

<dt>32. Do you stick to certain colours? Do you do that on purpose, or is it your subconscious?</dt>
<dd>Yes and yes. It's the template in the site generator...</dd>

<dt>33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?</dt>
<dd>Sometimes I do. I have a few other projects and things and at those times I let the website fend for itself.</dd>

<dt>34. Do you have many webmaster friends, or is it a solitary hobby?</dt>
<dd>Solitary generally.</dd>

<dt>35. Do people in your real life know about your website?</dt>
<dd>Yes.</dd>

<dt>36. Do you update your website very often? How often is "very often"?</dt>
<dd>Going from weekly to monthly. Sometimes more if I'm doing something big.</dd>

<dt>37. And the overall design, do you change that much? Why or why not?</dt>
<dd>Not really. I'm not that interested in graphic design, so after I got to a point I was happy with I stopped. Some of my subdomains look a bit different, while some use the same stylesheet.</dd>

<dt>38. Is your website more you-focused, hobby-focused, or outside world-focused?</dt>
<dd>A mixture of you-focused and hobby focused.</dd>

<dt>39. Do you do web design professionally?</dt>
<dd>It's an occasional part of my job.</dd>

<dt>40. If not, would you like to? And if you're comfortable answering, what do you do for work?</dt>
<dd>In some respects I already do that. I'm a software developer in a consultancy company that mainly focuses on law firms. So I tend to get a fair bit of variety.</dd>

<dt>41. Do you communicate with people by email very much?</dt>
<dd>Generally only in a professional or formal situation.</dd>

<dt>42. Some people reject social media and use websites as a replacement. Do you keep social media outside of your website?</dt>
<dd>Do you keep social media outside of your website?
Linkedin, but that's more of a CV for me than a social network. Reddit, but that's more of an anti-social network (tbh I wonder why I bother opening it sometimes).</dd>

<dt>43. How about instant messengers? Do you use a mainstream one like Discord or Telegram? Or something like Matrix? Do you avoid them?</dt>
<dd>WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Slack and Discord. I use them effectively as text messaging.</dd>

<dt>44. Do you listen to music while you work on websites? If so, what kinds of artists?</dt>
<dd>TV if it's in my own time.</dd>

<dt>45. Do you keep everything you make on one website, or do you have more than one?</dt>
<dd>I've been bringing the various sub repos back into the main site. I try to keep it on the main one, just so I'm not forever finding things.</dd>

<dt>46. On a similar note, do you keep to one topic on your site, or many?</dt>
<dd>Many. There's a tags index page and the completely abused Meta tag for when I just want a catch all one (boy I wonder what tag this got filed under).</dd>

<dt>47. Do you present your real self, or at least try? Or do you construct a persona on purpose?</dt>
<dd>I generally present my real self. Unless you're reading under the fiction tag, then all bets are off.</dd>

<dt>48. Have you ever made a good friend thanks to your website?</dt>
<dd>Not yet.</dd>

<dt>49. Are you happy with the way HTML and CSS currently work?</dt>
<dd>People who do not close a tag should get ten years in prison. Life sentences for those who write validators that tell people not to close tags. I had to stop the drop-downs on the NavBar animating as Edge and Chrome for Android stopped the NavBar working properly.</dd>

<dt>50. What are practices that you think people should avoid?</dt>
<dd>Making things inaccessible on mobile.</dd>

<dt>51. What about under-utilised practices, or things you think people should do more?</dt>
<dd></dd>

<dt>52. Do you use a lot of semantic HTML? Or are you guilty of generic structure?</dt>
<dd>I try to use semantic HTML when I can. But I am guilty of putting in div a lot.</dd>

<dt>53. Do you consider different browsers?</dt>
<dd>Do Edge and Chrome count?</dd>

<dt>54. Speaking of, what's your preferred browser? Convince your readers why they should use it.</dt>
<dd>I generally use Chrome. I know that I should use Firefox.</dd>

<dt>55. And what OS are you on?</dt>
<dd>Windows. I have a raspberry pi with Debian on it and an ancient MacBook. So all three major OSs.</dd>

<dt>56. Do you have a strong opinion on that, or do you just happen to use it?</dt>
<dd>No especially. I mostly use it so that I can play computer games. At some point I need to learn more about Linux.</dd>

<dt>57. Are your websites mobile-friendly?</dt>
<dd>Yes. I try to make sure that they work on a mobile screen and a PC screen.</dd>

<dt>58. What are your thoughts on autoplay?</dt>
<dd>Don't do it.</dd>

<dt>59. What are your thoughts on webrings? Are you in any?</dt>
<dd>I thought about it. You never quite know who your neighbour will be.</dd>

<dt>60. Do you have any web shrines? What do you like to see in that sort of page?</dt>
<dd>Not knowingly.</dd>

<dt>61. Are your websites "cliche", in your opinion?</dt>
<dd>Probably, the design is inspired by Bootstrap, so not exactly original.</dd>

<dt>62. What is your ideal website? Are you striving for that, or for something else?</dt>
<dd>One with a game on it. Every so often.</dd>

<dt>63. Are you an artist? Do you draw or design your own assets?</dt>
<dd>Sometimes. My logo was made in Stud.io</dd>

<dt>64. What are your favourite resource sites?</dt>
<dd>StackOverflow, MDN</dd>

<dt>65. Is there a habit you just can't get away from no matter how hard you try?</dt>
<dd>Closing every tag.</dd>

<dt>66. What's your biggest advice for a new webmaster?</dt>
<dd>Don't start a blog with how you made it. As generally that's the last post, although some follow it with a second post about how they moved their blog to another framework.</dd>

<dt>67. Do you keep all your styling in CSS? Or do you hard-code some?</dt>
<dd>CSS, although Astro is trying to persuade me to put it in style tags.</dd>

<dt>68. What do you think of frameset layouts?</dt>
<dd>Not sure I've knowingly seen one.</dd>

<dt>69. How about table-based layouts?</dt>
<dd>How about no!</dd>

<dt>70. Do you subscribe to the ideas of "one-column", "two-column" and "three-column" layouts? Do you use any of these?</dt>
<dd>I've used them a bit. 2+ columns don't really translate to mobile.</dd>

<dt>71. Do you spend longer on the HTML or the CSS?</dt>
<dd>CSS.</dd>

<dt>72. Have you ever made a page with no CSS? It's useful for your thoughts.</dt>
<dd>Well yes, but also no. Each page on the site might as well use the SSG's layouts.</dd>

<dt>73. Do you ever find yourself making layouts with nothing to put on them? Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?</dt>
<dd>Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?
Generally my site has been begging me to give it another layout or improve an existing one.</dd>

<dt>74. Would you consider yourself a beginner? Or advanced? Somewhere in the middle?</dt>
<dd>Middle of the road.</dd>

<dt>75. Do you have a habit of looking at the source code of websites you visit?</dt>
<dd>Generally not. But I might if I saw something interesting.</dd>

<dt>76. How did YOU learn how to make websites?</dt>
<dd>Computer club at secondary school started it. Then FreeCodeCamp.</dd>

<dt>77. Do you ever force elements to do things they're not supposed to?</dt>
<dd>Why do you think I have such disagreements about the HTML validator's opinion?</dd>

<dt>78. Thoughts on floating elements?</dt>
<dd>Generally they indicate that something has gone wrong.</dd>

<dt>79. When you're sizing stuff, what do you use first? Do you use px, em, %, or something else?</dt>
<dd>Px and rem.</dd>

<dt>80. Do you have a favourite font?</dt>
<dd>Times New Roman and Ariel.</dd>

<dt>81. Would you run a website with another person? How would that work?</dt>
<dd>I do at work.</dd>

<dt>82. Do you surf the Web to find new personal websites very often?</dt>
<dd>Sometimes.</dd>

<dt>83. Do you bookmark other people's websites? How would you feel knowing someone else bookmarked yours?</dt>
<dd>Occasionally I bookmark other people's sites. Embarrassed and a bit proud.</dd>

<dt>84. What do you want people to be most impressed with when they see your website?</dt>
<dd>How fast it loads and what a NavBar.</dd>

<dt>85. Are you interested in technology outside of websites? Do you collect?</dt>
<dd>I have a computer of each OS variety. I'm a 3D printer owner (impressive when it feels like working). At some point my VR headset needs some dusting off.</dd>

<dt>86. How often and for how long are you online?</dt>
<dd>Too long.</dd>

<dt>87. When it comes to your website, who is your target audience?</dt>
<dd>Me?</dd>

<dt>88. Have you ever been interested in XHTML?</dt>
<dd>HTML5 was new when I started learning...</dd>

<dt>89. Do you program in general? Have you ever written a program for use with or on your website, not counting simple JavaScript?</dt>
<dd>Yes for work. I have my XKCD reader app in React Native. For my Wordle stats I wrote a script to get those from a WhatsApp dump. There's a Power shell script I used to sort my photos.</dd>

<dt>90. Speaking of programs that help you make websites, what do you think of static site generators (SSGs)? Have you ever used one?</dt>
<dd>Yes. My site is in Astro now, previously Jekyll. My other site 11ty (I don't like that). My site started off as separate HTML pages and after needing to visit every file to update things I decided to employ the computer to do that for me.</dd>

<dt>91. Do you keep a hitcounter? Why or why not?</dt>
<dd>No. I assume that my audience is me, scrapers and people visiting by accident.</dd>

<dt>92. Do you frequent forums? Which ones?</dt>
<dd>The IndieWeb one.</dd>

<dt>93. Do you write your page content directly into the editor, or do you prepare it elsewhere, like a text document or a Word document?</dt>
<dd>Generally I start in Joplin and move it over. </dd>

<dt>94. Do you think you appear cool to others? A more accurate answer now: do other people ever say you're cool?</dt>
<dd>Probably not. Not that I can recall.</dd>

<dt>95. Are you embarrassed of your old work? Have you ever deleted everything out of shame?</dt>
<dd>Probably, you can always update it. Or conveniently forget about it.</dd>

<dt>96. Would you close down your website if you couldn't update it, or would you leave an archive?</dt>
<dd>I think it's already on some archive. As it's static it's not like I need to update it.</dd>

<dt>97. Do you reveal a lot about yourself on your website? Or are you more secretive?</dt>
<dd>I try not to. But you could probably put together a fairly detailed picture of me.</dd>

<dt>98. Are you willing to reveal who your best online friend is, and/or if they have a website?</dt>
<dd></dd>

<dt>99. And do you optimise the images on your website?</dt>
<dd>That's Astro's job now! Does making them webP count.</dd>

<dt>100. We're out of time! How do you feel after answering 100 questions? ....other than exhausted.</dt>
<dd>Glad it's over.</dd>
