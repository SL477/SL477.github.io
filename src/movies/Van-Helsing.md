If you like mad vampire movies then you'll love this one.
I assume that this was meant to be the start of a new franchise that never happened.

Wolverine plays "Hugh Jackman" playing Van Helsing, in his best non-Wolverine role.

The vampire off handedly slaughter their way through Transylvania, as they should.
Van Helsing has a mysterious past and is employed by the Vatican to hunt monsters, which he does with considerable enthusiasm.

We have Castle Frankenstein and Castle Dracula, as vampires have money.
We also get to see Dracula's summer palace in Budapest.

Dracula shows a blatant disregard for gravity, casually walking along the ceiling and his speed puts the weeping angels to shame.
