The start of the MCU.
Tony Stark builds his super suit and has a nice sunny holiday in Afghanistan.

It takes him a little time to get away from his crazy lifestyle and start becoming a superhero.

I liked Marvel's decision to ditch the whole secret identity when he announces "I am Iron Man."

Not sure the Iron Man suit is particularly realistic.
I see why in the second movie the villain makes robots instead.
